# Product demo and UI notes

The dashboard deliberately uses real record counts, not fabricated growth indicators or placeholder graphs. It is designed as a practical residence-office tool rather than a public marketing homepage.

## Staff demonstration route

1. Sign in as staff, review **Operations overview**, the attention queue and capacity by building.
2. Open **Applications** and allocate a room to a pending student.
3. Open **Rooms**, choose **Residents** on a room and inspect its current roster.
4. From **Housing changes**, approve or reject a transfer/move-out and confirm occupancy changes.
5. From **Service requests**, assign a case, reply to its student and resolve it with a note.
6. Publish a building announcement in **Announcements**, then archive it.
7. Show **Reports**, **Activity log**, CSV export, filters, and pagination as evidence.

## Student demonstration route

1. Register or sign in, see current room and notices on **Overview**.
2. Apply for a room through **Accommodation**; view review status and notes.
3. After allocation, request a transfer or move-out in **Housing changes**.
4. Submit and discuss a maintenance ticket in **Service requests**.
5. View residence-wide and building-specific **Announcements** and personal **Notifications**.
6. Open the command menu with **Ctrl+K**, and use mobile navigation at narrow widths.

## Visual language

The interface uses a quiet blue-green accent, a light institutional sidebar, readable table density, no decorative charts with invented data, explicit status tags, keyboard-accessible buttons, and a responsive layout. No external fonts, images or component CDNs are required.
